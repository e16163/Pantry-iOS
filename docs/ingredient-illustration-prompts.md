# Ingredient illustration prompts (the rest)

Your 100 ingredient illustrations are in the app. These 98 ingredients don't have their own yet, so they show their category illustration for now (same style, just less specific).

Make each one in ChatGPT with the style prompt below plus its line from the list, and save it with the exact file name shown (PNG). Then give the folder to Claude to add them, the same way as the first 100.

## Style prompt (paste first, every time)

```text
Flat vector-style food illustration of ONE ingredient, centered on a rounded-square tile in soft sage green (#D3E6DA) with large rounded corners, on a pure white square background with a small white margin around the tile. Bold simple shapes, no outlines, a limited palette of deep greens (#0B6B35, #0E2A1C), off-white (#F5F1E8), tomato red (#E2573A) and warm yellow where the food needs it, plus one soft dark-green oval shadow under the item. Slight 3/4 view, the item filling about 60% of the tile. No text, no labels, no brand logos, no hands, no plates unless listed. Square 1:1, 1254 x 1254 px.
```

Then add: `The ingredient is: <name>.` Batch a few at a time if ChatGPT allows, but keep one ingredient per image.
