# Pantry Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Pantry helps you cook with what you already have. It keeps your information on your iPhone, except for the things listed under "What goes online".

## What we collect

Pantry has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't keep your photos or know who you are.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your pantry and favorite recipes** are saved on your device only.
- **Fixes you make to a scan** (for example, changing "lemon" to "lime") are saved on your device so later scans get them right.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## What goes online

- **Photos you scan** are sent to Google's Gemini AI through our server on Cloudflare to find the ingredients in them. We don't store them. Google may keep and review them to improve its services, under [Google's terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Without a connection, the photo is read on your iPhone instead.
- **Your ingredient list** is sent to Google's Gemini AI through our server on Cloudflare when you ask for recipes, so it can write them. Without a connection, Apple Intelligence writes them on your iPhone instead.
- **Recipe names** are sent to our server on Cloudflare to make a picture of each dish. Pictures are stored and shared with everyone who sees the same recipe. Nothing about you is sent with them.

## Camera and photos

Pantry asks for the camera only when you choose to take a photo of your fridge or pantry. When you pick a photo from your library, iOS shows only the photo you choose; Pantry can't see the rest of your library.

## Siri and Shortcuts

If you add ingredients or ask what you can cook using Siri or Shortcuts, Pantry handles the request on your device. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Siri works.

## Children

Pantry doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
