# Pantry privacy policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Pantry keeps your information on your iPhone, except for what's listed under "What goes online".

## What we collect

Pantry has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't keep your photos, and we don't know who you are.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your pantry and favorite recipes.** Pantry saves these on your iPhone only.
- **Fixes you make to a scan**, like changing "lemon" to "lime". Pantry saves these on your iPhone so later scans get them right.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## What goes online

- **Photos you scan.** Pantry asks before your first scan. It then sends each photo through our server on Cloudflare to Google's Gemini AI, or to Groq when Gemini is busy, which lists the ingredients in it. We don't store your photos. Google may keep and review them to improve its services, under [Google's terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Groq handles them under [Groq's privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy). Without a connection, Apple Intelligence reads the photo on your iPhone instead, on iPhones that support it.
- **Your ingredient list.** When you ask for recipes, Pantry sends your ingredients through our server to Groq or Google's Gemini AI, which write the recipes. Without a connection, Apple Intelligence writes them on your iPhone instead.
- **Recipe names.** Our server looks up a stock photo of each recipe on Pixabay and asks Gemini or Groq whether the photo shows that dish. The server keeps each photo and shows it to everyone who gets the same recipe. Nothing about you goes with the recipe name.
- **Your IP address.** Our server sees it, like any website does, and uses it only to stop one device from sending too many requests. We don't store it or link it to you.

## Camera and photos

Pantry uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo of your fridge or pantry. When you pick a photo from your library, iOS gives Pantry only that photo. Pantry can't see the rest of your library.

## Siri and Shortcuts

When you add ingredients or ask what you can cook with Siri or Shortcuts, Pantry handles the request on your iPhone. Apple's privacy policy covers Siri itself.

## Children

Pantry has no accounts and stores nothing about anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
