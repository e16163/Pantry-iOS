# Pantry Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Pantry helps you cook with what you already have. It's built so that your information never leaves your iPhone.

## What we collect

Nothing. Pantry has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't send any of your data to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your pantry and favorite recipes** are saved on your device only.
- **Photos you scan** are read on your iPhone to find ingredients, using Apple's on-device tools. They are not uploaded and Pantry doesn't keep them.
- **Fixes you make to a scan** (for example, changing "lemon" to "lime") are saved on your device so later scans get them right.
- **Recipe pictures** made with Apple's Image Playground are created on your device and saved on it.
- **Recipes written by Apple Intelligence** are created on your device. Pantry doesn't use any online AI service.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## Camera and photos

Pantry asks for the camera only when you choose to take a photo of your fridge or pantry. When you pick a photo from your library, iOS shows only the photo you choose; Pantry can't see the rest of your library.

## Siri and Shortcuts

If you add ingredients or ask what you can cook using Siri or Shortcuts, Pantry handles the request on your device. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Siri works.

## Children

Pantry doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
